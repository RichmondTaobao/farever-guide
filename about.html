About Farever Guide

Farever Guide is an independent, fan-made editorial resource designed to help players find clear routes through classes, weapons, builds, exploration and dungeons.

Our purpose

We organize player questions into focused guides, distinguish official facts from community advice, and review patch-sensitive recommendations as the Early Access game evolves.

Editorial standards

  • Official sources are preferred for release details, features and updates.
  • Advice and rankings should be labeled as editorial or community-tested.
  • Statistics and mechanics should not be published until they can be verified.
  • Corrections are welcomed through the contact channel below.
