Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Farever Guide and its service providers process when you use this website.

Information processed automatically

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Hosting infrastructure may process standard request information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, timestamps and security logs. Review the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you select “Accept analytics” in our cookie notice. It helps us understand page views, approximate geography, device/browser information and interactions with the site. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies and process device, browser, IP-address-derived and on-site activity data. Google states that Analytics does not log or store individual IP addresses.

You may decline analytics or reopen “Cookie settings” in the footer. You can also clear this site's local storage in your browser to reset your choice. Learn how Google uses data from partner sites and use Google's available browser opt-out controls.

Local storage

We use local storage to remember your language, color theme and cookie preference. These choices remain on your device until you clear them.

Embedded content and external links

YouTube videos use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. Playing a video or following an external Steam, Google, GitHub or YouTube link may allow that provider to process information under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

Farever Guide does not currently serve advertising. If advertising is introduced, this policy and the consent mechanism will be updated before ads are enabled. Where required, a Google-certified consent management platform will be used.

Your choices and contact

You can decline analytics, clear local storage, use browser privacy controls, or contact RichmondTaobao@gmail.com with privacy questions. Do not send sensitive information.
